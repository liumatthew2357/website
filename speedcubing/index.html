I have taken part in some of the most prestigious speedcubing competitions worldwide. In 2019, I competed at the Canadian National Championship. In 2022, I competed in the first North American Championship officially recognized by the World Cube Association.

In 2023, I represented Canada at the US National Championship. With 1,028 competitors, it was the largest speedcubing competition ever held at the time, surpassing the previous World Championship.

I am also President of Waterloo Cube Academy, a service that teaches students how to solve Rubik's Cubes. Learn more
